# Code of conduct

This project follows the
[Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).
The full text is at that link. In short:

- Be respectful and assume good faith. Critique ideas and code, not people.
- No harassment, insults, sexualised language or imagery, or personal attacks.
- Do not publish anyone's private information without their explicit permission.
- Keep discussion on topic. This is a tool for one auction protocol, not a venue for
  token promotion or trading advice.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, commits, issues and pull requests that break
these rules, and may ban contributors who repeatedly or seriously break them.

Report a problem privately through a
[GitHub security advisory](https://github.com/cocoa-butter/cca-bid-planner/security/advisories/new)
on this repository, marked as a conduct report. Reports are kept confidential.
