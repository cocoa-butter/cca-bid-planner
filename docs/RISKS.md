# Bidder risks

Upstream's "Bidder responsibilities" section (`docs/TechnicalDocumentation.md` at
`v2.1.0`) lists the ways a CCA can be configured against bidders. This table maps each
risk to the check in `src/core/verify.ts` that covers it, or says that no check can.

| Risk                                                                                          | Check id                                                    | Severity           | What the tool can tell you                                                                                                                           |
| --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------ | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Floor price above fair value                                                                  | `manual`                                                    | —                  | Nothing. Fair value is not on chain. `inspect` shows the floor and what a full sale at the floor raises.                                             |
| `requiredCurrencyRaised` so high the auction never graduates                                  | `graduation-floor`, `graduation-high`                       | warning            | Compares the minimum raise with a full sale at the floor and flags anything above 200%. Every `plan` scenario reports whether the auction graduates. |
| Until graduation nothing exits, and a failed auction refunds everyone                         | —                                                           | —                  | `plan` and `simulate` model this: non-graduating scenarios show a full refund and no tokens.                                                         |
| Extreme start, end or claim blocks keep refunds or tokens out of reach                        | `blocks-ordered`, `claim-delay`                             | critical / warning | Checks block order and flags a claim delay over 50,000 blocks. That threshold is this tool's choice.                                                 |
| Honeypot, malicious or fee-on-transfer token                                                  | `manual`                                                    | —                  | Not checkable from the auction contract. Fee-on-transfer tokens are unsupported upstream.                                                            |
| `positionRecipient` withdraws liquidity right after the pool is created                       | `manual`                                                    | —                  | Not checkable from the auction contract. That role lives in Uniswap liquidity-launcher.                                                              |
| Tick spacing small enough to be gas-hostile, or to let an attacker stop the auction finishing | `tick-min`, `tick-gas`                                      | critical / warning | Spacing must be ≥ 2. Upstream recommends at least 1 bp of the floor.                                                                                 |
| Thin final block makes the final price, which seeds the pool, easy to manipulate              | `last-block-supply`, `steps-monotonic`                      | warning / info     | Flags a final block that releases under 1% of supply (this tool's threshold) and a schedule whose rate decreases.                                    |
| Low-decimal (< 6) token or currency loses value to rounding                                   | `decimals`                                                  | critical           | Checks both decimals.                                                                                                                                |
| Malformed schedule, floor or supply (the contract would not deploy)                           | `steps-valid`, `floor-min`, `floor-on-tick`, `total-supply` | critical           | Same rules the constructor enforces.                                                                                                                 |
| Validation hook rejects bids or needs hookData                                                | `validation-hook`                                           | info               | Flags any non-zero hook. `calldata` warns when the hook is set and no `--hook-data` is given.                                                        |
| Currency sent straight to the auction is lost                                                 | `manual`                                                    | —                  | `calldata` reminds you to fund bids only through `submitBid`, using Permit2 for ERC20.                                                               |
| Bid lands at or below clearing and reverts                                                    | —                                                           | —                  | `plan` only uses ticks strictly above the clearing price at the submission block. Clearing can still move before your transaction lands.             |

`verify` exits with code 3 when any critical check fails.
